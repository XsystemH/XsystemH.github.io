Chaoyi Huang (PiaoZY)

👋 Hi! I'm Chaoyi Huang (黄超逸), a fourth-year computer science undergraduate in the ACM Honors Class at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Yong Yu. I expect to graduate in June 2027.

🤖 I'm a research assistant at the University of Hong Kong. My work focuses on robot learning and tool use.

🌱 How can a robot tell which part of an unfamiliar tool to use? Once it starts acting, how can it tell whether the action worked? I study these questions through robot learning, simulation, and experiments on physical robots.
